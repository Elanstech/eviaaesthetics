FAQ

Common Questions

Who actually performs the IV therapy?
Every IV drip and injection at Eviaesthetics is personally administered or directly supervised by Dr. Evia A Nano, MD — a board-certified physician with more than 20 years of clinical experience. Unlike many drip bars and mobile services, you are never treated by an unsupervised technician. This is the single biggest difference between us and the rest.
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Eviaesthetics is a private, appointment-only suite — that's how Dr. Nano can give you her full attention without a crowded waiting room. Booking takes a minute online or by phone, and same-day appointments are often available. Just reach out and we'll find you a time that works.
How long does an IV drip session take?
Most IV drip sessions take 45 to 60 minutes from start to finish. NAD+ infusions are longer — typically 2 to 3 hours — because they're administered at a slower drip rate for optimal comfort. Vitamin injections take about 15 minutes.
How much does IV therapy cost?
Vitamin injections start at $50, custom IV drips start at $350, and NAD+ infusions start at $600. Pricing varies based on the specific formula, ingredients, and dose selected with Dr. Nano during your intake. You'll always know the price before anything begins — no surprises.
Is IV therapy safe?
IV therapy at Eviaesthetics is performed in a clinical setting under direct physician oversight using sterile, medical-grade equipment. A brief medical intake is completed before your first session to confirm suitability and rule out contraindications — exactly the kind of safeguard a pop-up drip bar can't offer.
How often should I get IV therapy?
Frequency depends on your individual goals. Many clients come weekly or biweekly for wellness maintenance, while others book seasonally or before events, travel, or recovery periods. Dr. Nano will recommend a cadence during your consultation.
Do you accept insurance?
We do not accept insurance for IV therapy or injections. We accept cash and all major credit and debit cards. Pricing is discussed openly before treatment with no surprise charges.
