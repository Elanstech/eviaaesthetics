FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SkinPen microneedling and how does it work?

SkinPen is an FDA-approved microneedling device that creates tiny, controlled micro-injuries in the skin. This triggers the body's natural wound-healing response, stimulating new collagen and elastin production. The result is smoother, firmer, more youthful skin. Treatments at Eviaesthetics take 60–90 minutes and are performed by Dr. Evia Nano, MD.

What does PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy treat?

PRP therapy treats fine lines, wrinkles, acne scars, uneven skin tone, skin laxity, and overall skin quality. By concentrating your body's own healing platelets and reinjecting them, PRP stimulates natural collagen production and tissue repair. At our Manhattan clinic, PRP sessions take approximately 90 minutes.

Can exosome therapy be combined with microneedling?

Yes! Exosome therapy is often used as a powerful add-on to SkinPen microneedling to amplify results. The exosomes are applied immediately after microneedling, penetrating deeply through the micro-channels for enhanced cellular regeneration. It can also be used as a standalone treatment.

How long is the recovery from microneedling?

Healing from SkinPen microneedling is typically complete in 3–5 days. Redness similar to a mild sunburn is normal for 24–48 hours. You can return to most activities the next day. We recommend avoiding makeup for 24 hours and using SPF 30+ daily for at least 2 weeks.

What is Profhilo and how is it different from fillers?

Profhilo is a bio-remodeling treatment, not a filler. While fillers add volume to specific areas, Profhilo uses highly concentrated hyaluronic acid to improve overall skin quality, hydration, and elasticity from within. It bio-stimulates collagen and elastin production for a natural, dewy skin improvement.

How much does a consultation cost?

An initial consultation at Eviaesthetics costs $200, which is fully credited toward your first treatment. Dr. Nano will evaluate your skin, discuss your goals, and create a personalized treatment plan. We accept cash, credit cards, and debit cards. Insurance is not accepted for aesthetic treatments.

How many microneedling sessions do I need?

Most patients see significant improvement after 3–4 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. However, the exact number depends on your skin concerns and goals. Dr. Nano will recommend an optimal treatment plan during your consultation. Maintenance sessions every 3–6 months help sustain results.

What is Salmon Sperm DNA therapy?

Also known as PDRN or polynucleotide therapy, this innovative treatment uses DNA fragments derived from salmon to promote cellular repair and regeneration. It improves skin texture, elasticity, hydration, and wound healing. Sessions take 60 minutes at our Manhattan clinic, with results visible in 3–4 weeks.
