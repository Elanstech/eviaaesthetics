Treatment Care Instructions

Everything You Need to Know Before & After Your Session

Pre-Treatment Preparation
  • Avoid retinoids and strong acids 5–7 days prior to treatment
  • No sun exposure or tanning 48 hours before appointment
  • Discontinue skincare acids (AHA, BHA, glycolic) for 1 week
  • Arrive with clean, product-free skin (gentle cleanser only)

  • Inform us of any active skin conditions or infections
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications if medically safe
  • Stay well-hydrated and maintain healthy skin barrier

  • Avoid retinoids and acids for 1 week post-treatment

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SkinPen microneedling and how does it work?

SkinPen is the first FDA-approved microneedling device. It creates tiny, controlled micro-injuries in the skin that trigger your body's natural wound-healing response, stimulating new collagen and elastin production. The result is smoother, firmer, more youthful-looking skin. Treatments at Eviaesthetics take 60–90 minutes and are performed personally by Dr. Evia Nano, MD.

What skin concerns does microneedling treat?

SkinPen microneedling effectively treats fine lines and wrinkles, acne scars, enlarged pores, uneven skin texture, stretch marks, sun damage, dull skin tone, and mild skin laxity. It works by stimulating your body's natural collagen and elastin production for gradual, natural-looking improvement.

Is microneedling painful?

Most patients describe microneedling as very tolerable. A numbing cream is applied 30 minutes before the procedure to minimize any discomfort. You may feel a light vibrating or scratching sensation during treatment. Dr. Nano adjusts the SkinPen's depth based on the treatment area and your comfort level.

How long is the recovery from microneedling?

Healing from SkinPen microneedling is typically complete in 3–5 days. Redness similar to a mild sunburn is normal for 24–48 hours. You can return to most activities the next day. We recommend avoiding makeup for 24 hours and using SPF 30+ daily for at least 2 weeks.

How many sessions do I need?

Most patients see significant improvement after 3–4 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. However, the exact number depends on your specific skin concerns and goals. Dr. Nano will recommend an optimal treatment plan during your consultation. Maintenance sessions every 3–6 months help sustain results.

Is SkinPen microneedling safe?

Yes. SkinPen is the first and only Class II microneedling device FDA-approved for improving the appearance of facial acne scars in adults aged 22 and older. At Eviaesthetics, all treatments are performed by board-certified physician Dr. Evia Nano, MD, in a clinical medical spa setting with the highest safety standards.

Dr. Nano will evaluate your skin, discuss your goals, and create a personalized treatment plan. We accept cash, credit cards, and debit cards. Insurance is not accepted for aesthetic treatments.
