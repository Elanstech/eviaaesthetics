Who These Treatments Are Best For

20s – Early 30s
  • Early prevention and skin hydration
  • Dull or tired-looking skin
  • Fine texture changes without volume loss
Best options: Skinvive or Profhilo
Mid-30s to 40s
  • Early signs of volume loss
  • Loss of firmness or elasticity
  • Mild sagging in cheeks or jawline
Best: Radiesse (structure), Skinvive (quality)
Late 40s to 50s
  • Noticeable volume loss
  • Hollowing in cheeks or temples
  • Skin laxity and collagen decline
Best: Sculptra (volume), Radiesse (lift)
60s & Beyond
  • Significant collagen loss
  • Facial thinning and sagging
  • Desire for natural-looking rejuvenation
Best: Sculptra + Radiesse combo with skin boosters

How to Choose What's Right for You

  • If your main goal is glow, hydration, and smoother skin: choose Skinvive or Profhilo.
  • If you want immediate lift and definition: Radiesse may be best.
  • If you prefer gradual, long-lasting results that improve over time: Sculptra is ideal.
  • Many patients benefit most from a personalized combination plan.

Consult with Dr. Nano to determine the safest and most effective treatment plan for your face.
