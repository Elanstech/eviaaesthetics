No. 6 Before You Visit

Questions, answered

Everything else, we'll cover in person — your consultation is private and judgment-free.

Who performs the treatment?

Every treatment is personally administered or directly supervised by Dr. Evia A Nano, MD — a board-certified physician with more than 20 years of clinical experience. You are never treated by an unsupervised technician.

How long does the relief last?

Most patients enjoy 4 to 12 months of dryness from a single treatment, depending on the area treated and your individual response. When the effect gradually wears off, treatment can simply be repeated.

Does it hurt, and is there downtime?

The treatment uses very fine needles and most patients describe only brief, minor pinches. There's minimal to no downtime — you can return to your normal day right away, with no bandages or recovery period.

How much does it cost?

Treatment ranges from $1,000 to $2,500 depending on the area or areas treated. Your exact pricing is confirmed during your consultation with Dr. Nano, with no surprises before anything begins.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Most patients begin to notice significantly reduced sweating within a few days, with the full effect settling in over about two weeks.

Do you accept insurance?

Treatment is offered on a private-pay basis. We accept cash and all major credit and debit cards, and pricing is discussed openly before treatment.
