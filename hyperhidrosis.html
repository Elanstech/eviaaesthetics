Treatment Care Instructions

Everything You Need to Know Before & After Your Session

Pre-Treatment Preparation
  • Shave underarms 24 hours before treatment (if treating underarms)
  • Avoid blood-thinning medications for 1 week if medically safe
  • Do not apply antiperspirant or deodorant on treatment day
  • Arrive with clean, product-free skin in the treatment area
  • Avoid alcohol consumption for 24 hours prior
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing on treatment day
  • Inform Dr. Nano of any medications or medical conditions
  • Eat a light meal and stay hydrated before your appointment
Post-Treatment Care
  • Avoid intense exercise or heavy lifting for 24 hours
  • Do not apply antiperspirant to treated area for 24 hours
  • Avoid hot baths, saunas, and steam rooms for 48 hours
  • Do not massage or rub the treated areas
  • Stay upright for 4 hours after treatment (avoid lying face down)
  • Results typically begin within 2–7 days of treatment
  • Full effect reached within 2 weeks of treatment
  • Schedule follow-up treatments every 4–12 months as needed

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is hyperhidrosis?

Hyperhidrosis is a medical condition that causes excessive sweating beyond what the body needs to regulate temperature. It can affect the underarms, palms, scalp, forehead, and other areas. It affects millions of people and can significantly impact quality of life, social confidence, and daily activities.

How does the treatment work?

The treatment uses FDA-approved targeted injections to temporarily block the nerve signals that tell your sweat glands to activate. Without these signals, the treated area stops producing excessive sweat. The injections are precisely placed using very fine needles, making the procedure quick and well-tolerated.

What areas can be treated?

At Eviaesthetics, we treat hyperhidrosis in the underarms (the most common area), palms of the hands, and scalp/forehead. During your consultation, Dr. Nano will evaluate your condition and recommend the best treatment areas based on your specific symptoms.

Is the treatment painful?

Most patients describe the treatment as very tolerable. A topical numbing cream or ice is applied beforehand to minimize any discomfort. The needles used are extremely fine, and each injection takes only seconds. The entire session is completed in 30–60 minutes, and most patients are surprised by how comfortable it is.

How long do results last?

Results typically last 4 to 12 months depending on the individual and treatment area. Most patients notice significant sweating reduction within 2–7 days, with full results by 2 weeks. Follow-up treatments are scheduled as needed to maintain your dryness and comfort.

Is this treatment FDA-approved?

Yes. The injectable treatment used for hyperhidrosis at our Manhattan clinic is FDA-approved specifically for the treatment of severe primary axillary hyperhidrosis (excessive underarm sweating) when topical agents have not provided adequate relief. It is one of the most well-studied and widely used treatments for this condition worldwide.

How much does treatment cost?

Hyperhidrosis treatment at Eviaesthetics Manhattan ranges from $1,000 to $2,500 depending on the treatment area and severity of the condition. An initial consultation costs $200, which is fully credited toward your first treatment. We accept cash, credit cards, and debit cards.

Is there any downtime?

There is virtually no downtime. Most patients return to normal activities immediately after the procedure. We recommend avoiding intense exercise for 24 hours, not applying antiperspirant to treated areas for 24 hours, and avoiding saunas or hot baths for 48 hours. You can return to work the same day.
