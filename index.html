Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered

Everything you'd want to know before your first visit

01 What is NAD+ IV therapy and do you offer it?

NAD+ IV therapy is an advanced anti-aging treatment that supports cellular regeneration and mitochondrial function. Yes — we offer NAD+ infusions in our Manhattan medical spa, administered personally by Dr. Nano. Sessions run roughly 90 minutes and start at $450.

02 Do you provide semaglutide weight loss treatments?

Yes. We offer medically supervised weight management programs including semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro). Every program includes comprehensive physician monitoring throughout your journey.

03 What aesthetic treatments do you offer?

A complete range of aesthetic treatments including Botox and dermal fillers (Juvéderm, Restylane), microneedling with SkinPen, PRP therapy, chemical peels, and custom IV therapy. All services are by appointment only and performed by Dr. Nano.

See full service menu
04 How much does Botox cost?

Botox starts at $16 per unit. Total cost depends on treatment area and units needed. We provide personalized consultations to determine exact pricing for your goals — no surprise charges.

05 Do you accept insurance?

We do not accept insurance. We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards. Pricing is discussed openly during your complimentary consultation.

06 How do I book a consultation?

Call (201) 639-4983, email info@eviaesthetics.com, or use our contact form. Consultations are complimentary and always private.
