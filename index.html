No. 6 Before You Visit

Questions, answered

Everything else, we'll cover in person — your consultation is complimentary and private.

What is NAD+ IV therapy, and do you offer it?

NAD+ IV therapy supports cellular regeneration and mitochondrial function. We offer it in our Manhattan spa, administered personally by Dr. Nano. Sessions run roughly 90 minutes, from $450.

Do you provide semaglutide weight-loss treatments?

Yes — medically supervised programs including semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro), with physician monitoring throughout.

What aesthetic treatments do you offer?

Botox and fillers (Juvéderm, Restylane), microneedling with SkinPen, PRP therapy, chemical peels, and custom IV therapy — all performed by Dr. Nano, by appointment.

Do you accept insurance?

We do not. We accept cash and all major credit and debit cards. Pricing is reviewed during your complimentary consultation.
