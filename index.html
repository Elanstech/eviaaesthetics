Manhattan Medical Spa Services - Eviaesthetics Dr. Evia Nano

NAD+ IV Therapy Manhattan NYC

Eviaesthetics offers premium NAD+ IV therapy in Manhattan. Our NAD+ drip treatments support cellular regeneration, anti-aging, and energy enhancement. Dr. Evia Nano administers NAD+ infusions in our luxury medical spa located at 65 W 36th Street, serving Midtown Manhattan, Times Square, and surrounding NYC areas.

Semaglutide Weight Loss Manhattan - Ozempic Wegovy Mounjaro

Medically supervised weight management programs featuring semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro) injections. Our Manhattan weight loss clinic provides comprehensive support for sustainable results under Dr. Evia Nano's expert care.

Botox and Dermal Fillers Manhattan NYC

Expert Botox injections starting at $16/unit and premium dermal fillers including Juvederm and Restylane. Specializing in natural-looking facial enhancement, lip fillers, cheek augmentation, and anti-aging treatments in Manhattan.

IV Therapy Manhattan - Vitamin Drips NYC

Comprehensive IV therapy menu including Vitamin C Glow, Hydration Plus, Energy Boost, and Immunity Shield treatments. Professional vitamin drips and nutrient infusions in our Manhattan medical spa.

Microneedling PRP Chemical Peels Manhattan

Advanced skin rejuvenation treatments including SkinPen microneedling, PRP therapy (vampire facial), and medical-grade chemical peels. Professional aesthetic treatments for skin renewal and anti-aging in NYC.
