Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a chemical peel take?

Most chemical peel sessions take between 30–45 minutes depending on the type and depth of peel. You can typically return to daily activities immediately after a light peel, while medium peels may require 1–3 days of recovery.

Is there any downtime?

Light peels have virtually no downtime — you may experience mild redness for a few hours. Medium-depth peels may cause peeling for 3–7 days. Dr. Nano will recommend the appropriate peel depth based on your schedule and skin goals.

How many sessions do I need?

Most clients see noticeable improvement after 1 session, but a series of 3–6 treatments spaced 2–4 weeks apart delivers optimal results. Dr. Nano will create a personalized treatment plan during your consultation.

What is mesotherapy?

Mesotherapy involves micro-injections of vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and hyaluronic acid directly into the middle layer of skin. This technique deeply nourishes the skin, improves elasticity, and provides intense hydration that topical products cannot match.

Are chemical peels safe for all skin types?

Yes — when performed by a board-certified physician like Dr. Nano, chemical peels can be safely customized for all skin types and tones. During your consultation, Dr. Nano will assess your skin and select the most appropriate formulation for you.

What does the $200 consultation fee cover?

Your initial consultation includes a comprehensive skin assessment, personalized treatment recommendations, and a customized care plan. The $200 fee is fully credited towards your first treatment, making it essentially free when you proceed.
